# D0065 — Give semantic proposals enough output and a generic residual relation

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: accepted
Amends: D0064

## Context

The first E0116 complete pass used `max_tokens=768`. The larger residual
replay at `max_tokens=1536` completed five previously failing long proposals,
including C7119, C7121, C925 and C949. The remaining abstentions were mostly
not output truncation: they described cross-clause conformance, quantified
“each” conditions, rank aggregates, restricted expressions and disjoint case
ranges that the primitive predicate vocabulary could not state. C1154 also
repeated an exhausted evidence query until its turn cap.

The comparison is regenerated by running the E0116 runner once over the
complete inventory and once with `--retry-from` on the residual `rows.jsonl`;
the exact commands and output directories are retained under
`.cache/runs/E0116/`.

## Decision

- Use `max_tokens=1536` as the normal bounded E0116 output budget. A future
  residual retry may use `2048`, but no run may use an unbounded response.
- Add one generic `relation` predicate constructor. Its first argument is a
  lowercase relation name and its remaining arguments are fact identifiers or
  nested predicates. It records a source-backed semantic relation or aggregate
  without adding a branch for each Fortran constraint.
- When an episode repeats the same evidence request twice, or the evidence
  budget is exhausted, the deterministic runner forces the next native call to
  `submit_semantic`. The repeated calls remain in the trajectory.
- Relation proposals are schema-accepted proposals only. They require the
  same source gate and still need an independent witness or later relation
  specialization before promotion into StandardIR wiring.

## Rejected

- Keep 768 tokens after observing structured-call truncation on long clauses.
- Add one predicate constructor per failed C-number.
- Silently invent or decompose cross-clause semantics in the deterministic
  harness.
- Let a model continue retrieving after the deterministic evidence budget is
  exhausted.

## Reversal condition

Revert or amend this decision if 1536 materially increases malformed output,
if `relation` becomes an escape hatch for ordinary primitive predicates, or if
independent witnesses cannot distinguish useful relation proposals from
unsupported placeholders.
