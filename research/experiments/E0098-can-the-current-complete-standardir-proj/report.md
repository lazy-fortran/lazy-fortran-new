## Wave normalization check

The remaining conflict is the shared prefix `r_digit_x2D_string '_'`. One
interpretation is an integer literal with a kind suffix. The other is a digit
string used as `r_kind_x2D_param` followed by the same separator in an enclosing
construct. Four uniform target rewrites were tested on the generated grammar:
left and right precedence on each of the two rules. Each resolved this
diagnostic and exposed a new conflict at `r_access_x2D_stmt` after `PUBLIC
READ`, where the parser cannot distinguish an access identifier from the next
statement without a statement boundary. Inlining the existing `r_kind_x2D_param`
choice at the integer-literal call site left the original conflict in place.

The first rewrite changes the conflict location without reducing the target
ambiguity. Continuing requires a sequence of target-specific precedence,
associativity, or conflict declarations. The bounded slice stops at this point.
The source closure remains unchanged at 519 composite records, zero unresolved
parser names, and zero independent rebuild differences. The tree-sitter export
remains a verification failure with 14 declared conflict groups after the one
mechanically witnessed addition.

## Interpretation

explicit production or one of the five source-linked lexical facts, and the
accepted assumed-expansion and erratum layers are present. The complete M2
milestone is not yet accepted because the roadmap currently requires all
generated grammar targets, including tree-sitter, to have no fatal structural
failure.

This is a target-export boundary, not evidence for an unresolved StandardIR
name. Under D0029, the selected production-parser portion of M2 is complete:
the specialized direct parser is the production target and tree-sitter is a
derived differential export. Further normalization would require
target-specific choices and is not on the production critical path.
