The analyzer makes no experimental model calls.

## Question

Can the E0078 composed profile drive a generated complete parser over real
source with AST and diagnostics?

## Method

The analysis reruns E0078, E0068 and E0063. It independently checks the
151-row E0078 composition profile, its source hash and its zero parser-target
count. It then generates a small facade whose profile constants carry that
composition identity and whose local operations are the generated complete
source acceptance and AST operations.

The facade is compiled with warnings as errors and executed against the five
files in the complete-source corpus and the five files in the AST corpus. A
mutation of the complete-source corpus is checked both by GNU Fortran and by
the generated diagnostic path.

The composed profile reaches the generated complete-parser facade without
adding parser aliases or handing structural wiring to a model. The facade
combines two already generated local operations. It demonstrates profile
composition, complete-source acceptance, AST construction and source-linked
diagnostics for the declared corpora. It is not yet a complete Fortran parser.

## Boundary

precedence operations over one combined real-source corpus, while preserving
the E0078 profile as metadata and keeping semantic checks outside parser
