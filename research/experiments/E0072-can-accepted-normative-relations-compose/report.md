# E0072 report

All values and evidence tables below are regenerated by
`research/experiments/E0072-can-accepted-normative-relations-compose/analyse.sh`.
The analyzer is pinned to `lazy-fortran-new` commit `e29f19f`, reruns E0043
and E0071 from their pinned inputs, and makes no experimental model calls.

## Question

Can accepted normative relations compose with D0019 records without turning
semantic facts into parser-only aliases?

## Method

The analysis reruns the D0019 typed record generator and the E0071
source-controlled adjudication. It retains the 182 base records and appends
all 37 accepted relation facts as separate provenance-bearing rows. The five
retained E0071 candidates are copied unchanged, and the 151-name E0070 residue
remains outside the accepted fact set.

The parser projection is generated from aliases and lexical facts with an
explicit parser target. Semantic-role facts remain in the composite fact table
but are not lowered into parser-only aliases. An independent traversal rebuilds
the projection map from the two predecessor tables. A mutation of one accepted
semantic fact must fail the fact-count gate.
