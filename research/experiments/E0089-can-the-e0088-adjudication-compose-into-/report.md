# E0089 report

All values and evidence below are regenerated by
`research/experiments/E0089-can-the-e0088-adjudication-compose-into-/analyse.sh`.
The analyzer makes no experimental model calls.

## Question

Can the E0088 C734 adjudication compose into an accepted successor semantic
ledger without changing the other E0087 rows?

## Method

The analyzer reruns E0087 and E0088. It applies the one-row successor policy to
C734, changing its state from `disputed` to `resolved`, removing the competing
predicate, and retaining its source phrase, source hash, fact requirements and
provided fact. Every non-C734 formalization row is compared byte-for-byte with
the E0087 output.

A separately maintained 23-row oracle checks statuses, forms, predicates,
fact sets, source evidence and adjudication states. The accepted dependency
graph is regenerated from resolved rows and checked topologically. Status and
predicate mutations are negative controls.
