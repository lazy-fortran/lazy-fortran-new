## Verdict

Not ready to merge.

The source StandardIR is not modified and the mechanism is generic, but the
target witness and preservation gates are incomplete.

## Findings

- A multi-alternative rejected alias can inherit the first alternative's
  lineage. Alternative reordering can therefore produce an incorrect
  `alias-lineage` witness.
- Witness validation does not compare source roles or representative
  provenance with the actual records. A mutated witness can validate.
- Export does not invoke witness validation before emitting a factored target.
- Tests are structural and EBNF-only; they do not cover alias chains, cycles,
  alternative order, all four formats or an independent preservation oracle.
- The target-normalization module grows from 1,137 to 1,676 lines, over the
  local 1,000-line cap.

`fo check`, `fo test` and `git diff --check` pass. The pre-existing lint/text
policy findings remain; no new target-normalization lint finding was observed.

This review is a verification failure, not a source-grammar defect. The
commit must not be merged until the witness identity, export self-validation,
preservation tests and module-size gate are repaired.
