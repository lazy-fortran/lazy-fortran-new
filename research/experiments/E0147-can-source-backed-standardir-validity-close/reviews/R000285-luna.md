# Luna review of current selected-root replay

The current files are a strong, traceable, mechanically generated syntactic
projection of the J3-24-007-backed StandardIR. They are not yet a complete
representation of the Fortran 2023 standard and are not ready as a usable
parser-generation baseline.

## Positive evidence

* The projection carries document, clause, page, source hash, byte offsets and
  lineage.
* The source-projection table covers 1,061 of 1,068 source alternatives and
  explicitly accounts for six omitted declared roots, corresponding to seven
  omitted alternatives; it reports no missing retained body.
* EBNF, ANTLR4, Bison and tree-sitter validate, and the negative control fails
  as intended.
* Lexical facts are separate, source-backed records. The JSONL lexer contract
  retains their provenance, including the processor-defined `rep-char` and
  Unicode spellings.

## Defects and gaps

* The generated Bison projection remains structurally unhealthy: 427
  shift/reduce and 2,266 reduce/reduce conflicts, plus four useless
  nonterminals and seven useless rules. These counts are target diagnostics,
  not proof that the normative grammar is wrong.
* The old validator reported zero useless rules because it counted the wrong
  warning phrase. The corrected validator now reports the Bison stderr counts.
* The seven omitted productions need their identities and dispositions exposed
  in the final report; omission must remain distinguishable from source loss.
* `%glr-parser` makes generation succeed despite ambiguity. It does not prove
  that a parser chooses standard-defined interpretations or produces correct
  diagnostics.
* The current gates do not yet exercise valid and invalid Fortran programs,
  source lexer behavior, diagnostics, constraints, semantics, or whether the
  omitted rules are exercised by a corpus.

## LFortran comparison

The pinned LFortran grammar has lower raw conflict counts because it uses
hand-factored designator and expression rules, precedence declarations,
actions, lexer integration and a parser-oriented start organization. Those are
expected target differences, not normative evidence. The large remaining gap
is nevertheless a real parser-target specialization problem that must be
measured and solved with generic transformations and independent witnesses.

StandardIR is genuinely stronger for auditable normative provenance and for
keeping the source-backed representation authoritative instead of importing
LFortran decisions. Those advantages must remain in the comparison ledger.

## Readiness

The selected export is ready for the next projection-analysis phase, not for
claiming a usable Fortran parser. The next gates are: reconcile all generated
metrics, expose every omitted disposition, classify conflicts, and add valid /
invalid corpus behavior with an independent oracle.
