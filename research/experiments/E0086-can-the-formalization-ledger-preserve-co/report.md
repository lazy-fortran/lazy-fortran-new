The analyzer makes no experimental model calls.

## Question

Can the formalization ledger preserve competing deterministic interpretations as
disputed without selecting parser structure?

## Method

The analyzer reruns the E0081 constraint inventory over the pinned J3/24-007
canonical text and joins extracted continuation lines. Three policy rows then
select longer clauses: two receive one normalized predicate each, while C734
retains two competing negative-disjunction scope candidates as `disputed`.
The other 284 inventory records remain unresolved.

The independent oracle checks the selected statuses, source evidence,
predicate candidates and fact sets. Accepted dependencies are derived only
from resolved rows, and their topological order is checked. A source mutation
and a predicate mutation are both rejected.

The slice demonstrates that a deterministic ledger can retain disagreement as
data. The disputed predicate is preserved with both candidates, but it does
not contribute accepted wiring or dependency facts. The experiment therefore
keeps adjudication separate from structural generation.

## Boundary

E0086 exercises a real `disputed` outcome, but only in a preselected three-row
slice. It does not adjudicate which C734 candidate is normative, and it does
not formalize the remaining 284 constraints. The next slice should broaden
the candidate-preserving inventory and define the adjudication evidence needed
before any disputed row can become an accepted StandardIR fact.
