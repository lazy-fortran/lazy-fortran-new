- The complete trace contains 4,871 rows: 4,069 mapped, 717 suppressed and 85

  target relations in the full trace, and all 124 rule-deduplicate rows have
  one; sequence-collapse and optional-wrapper-removal suppressions do not
  require a retained target under the current relation contract.

## Defects and open boundary

The previous audit's “95 one-to-one joins” wording was too strong. Its join key
omitted `source_node_kind`, and even after that correction the input mapping
contains duplicate structural keys. The trace also does not carry the
candidate derivation kind, so the audit cannot claim that distinct candidate
rows have distinct target relations.

The result supports only this narrower statement:

> Each of the 95 candidate rows has one deterministic lookup result under the
> current structural join, and the nine selected rule-deduplication rows retain
> source/target provenance.

Before separator insertion, the system must either carry the complete candidate
identity through the generic correspondence trace or coalesce duplicate
derivations into one structural boundary while retaining all derivation
evidence. The choice must be generic and independently checked; no rule-number
or candidate-kind exception is justified by this review.
