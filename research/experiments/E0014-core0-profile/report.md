All counts, hashes and membership sets below are regenerated by
`research/experiments/E0014-core0-profile/check-core0-closure.sh`. The gate
uses `standard-new` at `17487dd`, the E0013 StandardIR artifact and the human
root records committed with the experiment definition.

The production tool emits one dependency record per unique R-number, one root
record and one closure-member record per selected rule, plus a summary. The
20 repeated R-number occurrences remain represented by their `occurrences`
field after identical lhs/reference shapes are checked; their separate source
locations remain in the input StandardIR artifact.

An independent awk graph walk over the input `(lhs ...)` and `(ref ...)` nodes
selected exactly the same 345 R-numbers. The 249 unresolved names are retained
and counted; they are names used in grammar notation that have no corresponding
lhs rule in this syntax corpus, not silently invented dependencies.

This closes the mechanical dependency-closure slice. It does not yet establish
that `core0-v1` is the intended supported-language profile. Broad standard
aggregates such as `action-stmt` make the positive closure reach syntax for
features the whitepaper initially excludes, including legacy transfer,
statement functions, block data, FORMAT machinery, coarrays and polymorphic
constructs. D0015 records the resulting boundary: reachability stays complete,
while feature eligibility and safe alternative pruning are a separate derived
layer.
