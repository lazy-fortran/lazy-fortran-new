## Bidirectional comparison

StandardIR's genuine advantages are source rule/page/byte-span/hash/lineage
provenance, preservation of normative categories and optionality, and a clean
boundary that keeps parser actions and implementation extensions out of the
normative representation.

The pinned LFortran reference's genuine advantages are executable lexer
integration, typed token payloads, precedence declarations, AST actions, an
explicit conflict budget, and hand-factored parser-oriented expression and
designator rules.

This review is differential evidence, not a language-equivalence proof. The
reference remains an oracle/comparison input; StandardIR remains the normative
source.
