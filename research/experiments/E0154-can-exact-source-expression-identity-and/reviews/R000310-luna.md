# R000310 Luna review of the failed E0154 replay

## Verdict

The replay is a useful, provenance-rich partial syntax projection, but it is
not yet a faithful representation of the Fortran 2023 standard. The failed
identity gate is correctly failed.

## Strengths

- StandardIR retains normative RHS structure plus J3 document, rule, page,
  byte span and document hash.
- All four outputs are mechanically generated from the selected `program`
  profile and retain per-alternative provenance.
- Duplicate source occurrences such as R1404 and R1416 retain both source
  spans in target lineage.
- The outputs preserve visible alternatives such as Unicode operators and
  character-literal forms, even where their identity witnesses are wrong.

## Defects

- The independent witness expects 1,068 source alternatives and covers only
  1,052 in each format.
- UTF-8 identity is wrong for the en dash/right-single-quote family, including
  R1010, R712, R724, R773, R774, R775 and R868.
- Long merged helper provenance is truncated: R901 has a 61-character final
  hash and R902 has five lineage entries but four hashes.
- R401/R402/R403 generated facts are labelled with source-expression hashes
  despite having no normative RHS in the generated fact record.
- Normalization removes the optional `specification-part` from R1404/R1416
  because the referenced nonterminal is nullable. This may preserve language,
  but the target body differs from the source and no independent
  normalization witness currently proves that transformation.

## Target gaps and unjustified claims

ANTLR4, Bison and tree-sitter acceptance is only target-file validity. Bison
still reports 427 shift/reduce and 2,266 reduce/reduce conflicts. The selected
root omits six declared roots. There is no executable lexer/runtime, typed
semantic-value contract, generated precedence policy, AST action layer or
positive/negative language corpus in this replay.

The replay does not justify claims of exact target-RHS preservation, complete
Fortran 2023 coverage, language equivalence, executable parser quality or
superiority to LFortran. StandardIR's genuine advantages remain its
normative provenance, duplicate-occurrence lineage and one-source/four-format
projection; LFortran's genuine advantages remain its executable lexer,
typed values, precedence/actions, parser factoring and conflict policy.
