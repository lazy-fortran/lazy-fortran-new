## Question

precedence coverage?

## Method

The analysis reruns E0079 and E0067. It independently checks the 151-row E0078
profile and its zero parser-target count. It generates a small facade whose
profile constants carry that identity and whose local operations delegate to
the generated expression-coverage operation.

The facade is compiled with warnings as errors and executed against nine
expression witnesses across six pinned real-source files. It checks precedence
roots, node-kind counts, source references, child links, known and unknown
queries, and an unsupported-operator mutation.
