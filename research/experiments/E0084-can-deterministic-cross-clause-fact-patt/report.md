## Question

Can deterministic cross-clause fact patterns formalize a broader Core 0
constraint slice without parser-specific branches?

## Method

The analyzer reruns the E0081 constraint inventory over the pinned J3/24-007
canonical text. It matches six predeclared constraints whose wording requires
facts from more than the local syntax occurrence: accessibility and type
membership, conditional module nature, alternative procedure membership,
declaration order or use association, and uniqueness.

Each match receives a subject, applicability, required facts, provided facts
and normalized predicate. The other 281 constraints retain `unresolved`
status. A separate oracle checks source phrases, predicates and fact sets. The
required and provided facts form a dependency graph, and every edge is checked
against a deterministic topological order. Source and predicate mutations are
required to fail.

## Boundary

E0084 extends the mechanical boundary from local predicates to cross-clause
fact requirements without adding parser-specific branches. The next slice
should test constraints with longer alternatives and source references, where
the normalized fact vocabulary may need an explicit unresolved or disputed
outcome. Model escalation remains deferred until that boundary is measured.
