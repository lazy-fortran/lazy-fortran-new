## Adjudication

This is a useful audit of representation, but not evidence that the grammar is
equivalent to Fortran 2023.

- The seven skipped emitted bodies are explicit and missing is zero, so source
  identity and emitted-body coverage are correctly separated.
- The corrected feature rows are structurally consistent. Every normative
  feature in the fixed matrix except `SELECT TEAM` appears in all four
  generated formats. `SELECT TEAM` is marked `neither`, not as a selected-root
  omission.
- `both` means present in at least one generated and at least one reference
  format, not present in every format. Feature presence is lexical, not
  syntactic or semantic; the `DO CONCURRENT` row uses a `CONCURRENT` terminal
  witness and cannot establish behavior.
- StandardIR's advantage is exact source-alternative identity: 1,068 expected
  and 1,068 covered, with equal generated lineage sets and provenance-oriented
  accounting. Reference advantages are independent comparative evidence and
  implementation-oriented organization; their head counts are not directly
  comparable to StandardIR alternatives.

Overall, normative representativeness is strong at the selected source/profile
and projection level, but incomplete as a claim about the whole language:
selected-profile scope, skipped witnesses, lexical-only feature checks, and
absent semantic/acceptance evidence remain material limitations.
