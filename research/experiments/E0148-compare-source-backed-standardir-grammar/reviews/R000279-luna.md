# Luna review of E0148/R000001

Verdict: qualified failure. The comparison is directionally correct, but it
must not be called an audit-clean exhaustive differential grammar study.

## Confirmed

- F001 and F002 are real target-projection defects. The source evidence keeps
  U+2013 and U+2019, but the generated grammar preserves them as target
  terminals instead of mapping them to canonical Fortran source spellings.
  F001 also covers the R868 range separator; it is not necessarily an
  arithmetic operator there. F002 covers BOZ delimiters as well as character
  literals, and should be described as typographic quote-delimiter
  normalization. LFortran corroborates the source spellings but is not the
  normative authority.
- F003 is a target-hygiene gap. Current output retains separate provenance for
  duplicate alternatives; it should be described as duplicate alternatives
  without one merged multi-lineage alternative, not as lost lineage.
- F004 is a selected-root/export gap. The all-roots wrapper emits 500 of 502
  declared roots; the two omitted roots are the explicitly skipped
  semantic-only `xyz-list` and `scalar-xyz` records.
- F005 is a selected-root reachability/pruning gap. The replay reports 10
  useless nonterminals and 514 useless rules.
- F006 is a proof gap, not an observed language defect: body-bound provenance
  and parser-generator acceptance do not prove language preservation under
  normalization.
- F007 is correctly classified as an expected LFortran difference. Its
  extensions and implementation factoring must not be imported into
  StandardIR.

## Required correction

F009 must state that the analyzer is exhaustive only over its declared token,
duplicate and Bison-replay dimensions. Its LFortran anchors and adjudication
are bounded manual evidence, not a generic pairwise body comparator. Unknown
Unicode must fail closed. D0091 reconciles the older D0042 prohibition with
D0090 while preserving the exact source facts.

No additional current StandardIR source-body defect was found in the reviewed
E0147/R000016 evidence.
